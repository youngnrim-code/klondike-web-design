Our mission

The whole store, brought to your door.

Running a small store has never been harder — labor is tight, guests expect speed, and technology is a maze. We chose the Beaver Store Lab platform because it does what scattered vendors can't: one well-designed system for the entire floor. Our job is to bring it to you, set it up, and stand behind it.

As Beaver's distributor, we make that lineup approachable for owners who never asked to become IT managers — from first-time founders to seasoned operators modernizing a lifelong business.

Our story

Why we became Beaver's distributor.

It started at the counter

We watched owners juggle a kiosk, a separate POS and a payment terminal that never agreed on the numbers. There had to be one system.

We found the right platform

Beaver Store Lab had already unified ordering, payments and the kitchen on one backend — a single menu engine syncing every device in real time. We didn't need to reinvent it; we needed to deliver it well.

We built the service around it

Consultation, nationwide installation, staff training and 365-day support — the parts that turn great hardware into a store that simply works.

Today & ahead

As Beaver's distributor we bring the full lineup to owners across the country — kiosks, barrier-free accessibility, table & QR ordering, payments and unattended-store systems — and we keep growing with them.
